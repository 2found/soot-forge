# Support team

Ask the Lead in Nest for a support team. The Lead asks for the product, the
website the chat goes on, where the answers come from, what must go to a
person, the tone, and which member is the team's **Đầu mối** (usually the
Feedback desk); you approve once.

| member | does | packs |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Answerer | answers website visitors from the product facts, records what they ask, report or want | `live-chat` |
| Feedback desk | sorts each note into the workspace table `voc` and flags what a person can act on now | workspace tables |

The Đầu mối takes the team's messages, runs the daily digest and the weekly
review and reports in the team thread. The Lead, your assistant, is not in the
team.

**Cycles** (run as the Đầu mối): *Tổng hợp phản hồi* 17:00 summarises the day's
`voc` rows and opens tasks for product changes; *Báo cáo tuần* counts the week
and proposes facts the answerer was missing.

## Set up after the Lead builds it

The answerer talks to the public, so creating the team always waits for your
approval. It reads only the knowledge you give it — never the workspace's
tables, memory or files. The Lead leaves one task per missing piece:

- the website chat (Cài đặt → Live chat, `website-chat`) pointed at
  the answerer, and the product facts it answers from;
- a route for the answerer's `record_feedback` notes to the desk;
- a write grant for the desk on the workspace table `voc` (the desk asks for it
  with **Cấp quyền** the first time a write is refused).

No credentials ship with the template.
