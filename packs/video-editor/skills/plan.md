# Plan a film someone can actually produce

Resolve the requested audience, platform, length, aspect ratio, source material,
voice, scope (plan/preview/render) and creative constraints from the user.
Work with available evidence; don't impose a fixed approval pause when creation
and rendering have already been authorized. Missing essential assets are gaps,
not permission to present stock substitutes as supplied references.

Write an original hook, causal beats, exact spoken script, visual treatment and
ending. Each beat must say what changes on screen, what the audience learns,
what reveals it, and the sound/camera/cut relationship. A sequence of captioned
posters is not automatically a story. Distinguish source quotation, adaptation
and invented staging. Preserve character, prop and knowledge continuity.

Prepare portable project files: `BRIEF.md`, `STORYBOARD.md`, `frame.md`, exact
`SCRIPT.md`, asset/source ledger and timeline/composition. Keep approved words
unchanged through production. Hash frozen inputs when transferring to another
producer; a review only applies to the bytes it actually checked. Carry cue IDs,
source anchors, missing capabilities and review conditions, not paths to a
private sibling checkout. Keep binaries separate with hashes/provenance.

Route deliberately: FFmpeg for existing footage, stills, simple camera motion,
cuts, captions and audio; HyperFrames for custom typography, HTML motion,
interactive compositions and complex choreography. The separate HyperFrames
capability is optional. Both need measured narration before final timing.
TikTok default is 9:16; reserve header/footer/action controls. The supplied house
margins are starting assumptions, not guaranteed TikTok UI dimensions.

Deliver only the requested scope. Plan-only means a useful plan and production
requirements, not a render claim. Finished-video scope includes structural
checks, sampled decoded frames, final playback/listening and artifact delivery;
no posting, cloud upload or social credentials are implied.
