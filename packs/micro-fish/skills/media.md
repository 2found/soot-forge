# Video, ad, campaign and marketing-strategy simulations

Use `kind: video` for a single video or ad, `marketing` for a campaign or strategy.

## Stimuli

- **Video**: the script with timestamps, what is on screen in each shot, on-screen text, sound/music, length, the platform (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts, YouTube long-form) and the caption. The first 3 seconds decide most of the result, so describe them exactly. If only a file exists, ask for the script or build a shot list from a contact sheet when the video-editor pack is granted.
- **Ad**: the creative, headline, body, call to action and where it is shown.
- **Campaign or strategy**: the message, audience, channels with cadence, offer, budget split and the first three pieces of content. Personas react to what they would actually see in their feed, so add one sample post per channel rather than the strategy deck alone.
- **A/B hooks or edits**: one stimulus per hook or cut, identical after the part that differs.

## Questions worth asking

- Would they stop scrolling? (`attention`) Would they finish it? (`watch_through`, 0–100)
- Would they share it, and to whom? (`share`, the public `post`)
- Does it feel like an ad? (watch the `ad-feel`, `fake`, `cringe` objection tags)
- Is the brand remembered and liked, or resented?
- Which market or segment carries it, and which one mocks it in the comments?

## Population hints

Mix heavy and light users of the platform, creators who comment, people who never engage, the target buyer, and people outside the target who will still see it. Give each persona `media` habits that match the platform and set `influence` higher for the few who post a lot. For VN, use real platform culture (TikTok comment register, Zalo groups, Facebook fan pages); for US/EU, vary between platforms and ad tolerance.

## Reading the numbers

- `attention_mean` is the hook; `watch_through_mean` is retention; `share_rate` and the posts are spread.
- A video with high attention and low watch-through has a hook that promises something the body does not deliver.
- In social rounds, look at `influential_posts`: the comment that moved people is often the comment the real video will get.
- Brand-safety or platform-policy findings from the expert panel outrank crowd enthusiasm.
